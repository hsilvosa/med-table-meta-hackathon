Medication table room

A practice room: photograph a table of medicines as a caregiver would, and see how the planner reads it.

  1. 1
    Walk to a table. Use the Table buttons, or W A S D and the mouse.
  2. 2
    Hold the phone over it. Packs are read from a code about 1 cm wide, so keep the phone 50 cm or closer. The bar at the top turns green when the distance is good.
  3. 3
    Take the photo. The planner opens on the right and reads it. It then guides you in four steps: the medicines it found, the doses you choose, a check of the plan, and a printable calendar.

This is a stage set: flat cut-outs of the official AEMPS photos, each with a Data Matrix sticker generated from its real national code (C.N.). Table 1 also holds a fictional medication sheet. It shows the flow and measures the optics; it does not prove how printed codes read on real packs.
