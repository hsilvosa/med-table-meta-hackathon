med-table

A medication planner for caregivers. Each medicine box is identified from the code printed on it and checked against Spain's official medicines database (AEMPS CIMA). The caregiver enters the doses from the prescription, and the tool prints a calendar, a weekly pill organiser guide and phone reminders.

The 3D room works best on a computer with a mouse.

Taking the photo in the 3D room

  1. On the left, choose Table 1, Table 2 or Table 3.
  2. Press Raise the phone. The view becomes the phone camera, 90 cm above the table. Hold still: green frames mark the boxes whose code was read, amber ones the boxes without a readable code.
  3. Press the red Take the photo button. The planner opens on the right with the medicines found.
  4. In the planner, choose the doses, tick each medicine, check the plan and download the calendar.

Keys: W A S D walk, mouse wheel zooms, R and F raise or lower the phone, Space takes the photo, Tab shows or hides the planner.

Without the 3D room

Open the planner, then Demo photos and Load a demo photo.

This is a public demo on a shared server: use the demo data only, and do not upload photos of real prescriptions or personal data. It is a planning aid, not medical advice. The voice, the Muse Spark and SAM 3 features and the camera page run only in the local installation (source code). Medicine data and photos: AEMPS CIMA, cima.aemps.es.
