A verified medication plan from a photo of the boxes

A planner for caregivers. Each medicine box is identified from the code printed on it and checked against Spain's official medicines database (AEMPS CIMA). The caregiver enters the doses from the prescription, and the tool prints a calendar, a weekly pill organiser guide and phone reminders.

Taking the photo in the 3D room

The panel on the left of the room follows the same steps.

  1. Choose a tablePress Table 1, Table 2 or Table 3.
  2. Raise the phoneThe view becomes the phone camera, 90 cm above the table. Hold still while the boxes are marked.
  3. Take the photoThe planner opens on the right with the medicines found.
  4. Plan the dosesChoose the doses, tick each medicine, check the plan and download the calendar.

W A S D walk · mouse wheel zooms · R F raise or lower the phone · Space takes the photo · Tab shows or hides the planner. Without the room: open the planner, then Demo photos and Load a demo photo.

Read from the box

The Data Matrix or the national code printed on each pack is decoded and its check digit validated.

Checked with AEMPS CIMA

A box counts as recognised only when the official database knows its national code. Expired packs are left out of the plan.

Every dose is the caregiver's

No amount is filled in: each dose is chosen from the doctor's paper and ticked medicine by medicine.
