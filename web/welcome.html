med-table

Photograph the medicine boxes on the table, get a medication plan you can trust. Each box is identified from the official code printed on it and checked against Spain's medicines database (AEMPS CIMA); the caregiver confirms every dose; the plan becomes a large-print calendar, a weekly pill organiser guide and phone reminders.

Not medical adviceA planning aid for caregivers. It never decides a dose and is not a medical device: every dose comes from the prescription and is confirmed by the caregiver, and every warning quotes its official source for the pharmacist to review.
A shared public demoUse the demo photo or the 3D room. Do not upload photos of real prescriptions or of anything showing a name, address or ID number: pictures are processed on a shared server. Photos taken in the 3D room are kept only for your own planner and deleted after an hour. All medicines shown are public AEMPS CIMA data; the medication schedule in the room is fictional.

In the 3D room

  1. Press Table 1, then Phone over the table.
  2. Hold still: green boxes appear on the codes that are read, amber on a box with no code.
  3. Press Take the photo. The planner opens with the medicines found; choose the doses, tick each one, check the plan and download the PDFs.

Keys: W A S D to walk, mouse wheel to zoom, R and F to raise or lower the phone, Tab to show or hide the planner. The room is a stage set: each medicine is a cut-out of its official CIMA photo carrying a Data Matrix generated from its real national code.

In this online version the voice, the reading of the doctor's schedule with Muse Spark and SAM 3 are turned off, and so is the camera page; they run in the local installation. Source code, documentation and the Meta models used: github.com/hsilvosa/med-table-meta-hackathon. Medicine information and photos: AEMPS CIMA, https://cima.aemps.es.
